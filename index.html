02 / Optional network lookup

Your public-facing connection

Your browser cannot normally read its public IP address directly. You can request a lookup from an external IP-information provider. That provider will receive your request and can see your IP address.

Nothing happens until you click.
03 / The invisible profile

What websites may learn

A single visit can expose technical and contextual details. Combine them over time and they can become useful for analytics, fraud detection, advertising or fingerprinting.

01 / CONNECTION

Network & location

IP address, approximate region, internet provider and connection metadata. IP location is an estimate, not a live GPS position.

Your device profile

Browser, operating system hints, screen dimensions, language, time zone, touch support and other browser capabilities.

Pages visited, clicks, scroll depth, time on page and referral information may be recorded by the site or its embedded services.

04 / STORAGE

Cookies & storage

Cookies and browser storage can remember identifiers and preferences. Their availability depends on browser settings and consent rules.

06 / PERMISSIONS

Camera, microphone and location access generally require permission. This page does not request them and cannot silently turn them on.

05 / Important limits

What this page cannot magically know

A website does not automatically get access to your passwords, private photos, messages, contacts, exact GPS location or files. Access usually requires you to submit information, grant permission, install software or use a compromised service. Claims otherwise are usually scare tactics wearing a hoodie.
